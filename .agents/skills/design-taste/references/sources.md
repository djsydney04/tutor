The [design index](design-index.md) holds the seven user-selected visual
references and their inspection status. Those pages inform reference study,
not permission to reuse brand assets.

The local workflow deliberately keeps established product conventions,
accessibility, and useful familiar patterns. It has no mandatory font, palette,
framework, theme, animation package, or universal ban on shapes or punctuation.
These choices require evidence from the target product.

If importing upstream files later, verify the exact revision and applicable
license, retain required notices, and review the actual instructions before use.
